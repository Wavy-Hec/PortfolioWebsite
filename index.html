06 VLM + RL · MuJoCo · Qwen3.5-9B · PPO

VLM-Guided Quadruped Navigation

A frozen vision-language model steers and a PPO policy walks: a Unitree Go2 in MuJoCo with one RGB camera. The VLM could point at a target to within about 2.5°. It could not judge distance: asked when to stop, it was right 8 to 14% of the time, and reading the bottom edge of its bounding box instead reached 79 to 86%. Accepted at IEEE ICMLA 2026.

The paper assumes the target stays in view. The follow-up gives the navigator a memory it can revise, so it can search for a target it has lost.

What it found → The follow-up → GitHub: VLM navigation ↗

10 PPO · MuJoCo · Isaac Sim

Go2 Locomotion

PPO policies that turn a speed and a heading into joint torques for the Go2’s twelve motors, trained in MuJoCo and Isaac Sim, with state autoencoders that cut compute cost and sped up training. The sketch below takes the same two commands.

A Unitree Go2 quadruped robot drawn as a kinematic sketch: its twelve joints and legs inside a dotted silhouette, trotting in place. The tags name its hardware: a 4D LiDAR L2 and a wide-angle camera at the head; an IMU at the trunk’s center; a thigh and a calf of 0.213 meters each; twelve joint motors: hip, thigh, and calf on each of the four legs. It weighs about 15 kilograms and stands about 70 by 31 by 40 centimeters (unitree.com).

第四章 About

About

Most of what I build comes down to one loop: a machine looks at the world, then decides what to do. At UTRGV’s Machine Intelligence Lab that machine is a Unitree Go2, learning to walk and to find things. At Idaho National Laboratory it was a power grid’s sensors, and for fun, a poker table and a chessboard.

In summer 2026 at the Air Force Research Laboratory I studied how vision-language models handle multi-camera video. The internship has ended, but I’m still working on that research, and a manuscript is in preparation.

When I’m not training agents, I train myself: taekwondo and hiking, slowly working up to summiting a real mountain.
