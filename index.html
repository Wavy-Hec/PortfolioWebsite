// about

About me

👋 Hey there! I'm Hector, a Master's student in Computer Science at The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. My passion is combining reinforcement learning, computer vision, and vision-language models, with a focus on autonomous navigation and locomotion for robotics.

Right now I'm an AI/ML research intern at the Air Force Research Laboratory, where my focus is deepfake research and multi-camera, multi-agent perception. Day to day I build RL pipelines and train agents in simulation with LocoMuJoCo and NVIDIA Isaac Sim — mostly locomotion, perception, and getting policies to transfer from sim to real.

Raiden trained for the real thing entirely in VR. My agents do the same — policies learned in simulation, then carried across the sim-to-real gap.
