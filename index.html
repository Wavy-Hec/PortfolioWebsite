// about

About me

I'm Hector, a CS master's student at UTRGV. I work on getting robots to move and to understand what they're looking at: reinforcement learning for legged locomotion, computer vision, and vision-language models for navigation.

This summer I've been an AI/ML research intern at the Air Force Research Laboratory, studying how vision-language models handle multi-camera video. The short version: when does it actually help to fuse camera feeds, and when does it not. I'm continuing that work with AFRL past the internship. Back at school I build RL pipelines in LocoMuJoCo and NVIDIA Isaac Sim and train locomotion policies meant to survive contact with real hardware.

Raiden trained for the real thing entirely in VR. My agents do the same — policies learned in simulation, then carried across the sim-to-real gap.
