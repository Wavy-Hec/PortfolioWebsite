// about

About me

👋 Hey there! I'm Hector, a Master's student in Computer Science at The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. My passion is building intelligent systems that combine reinforcement learning, computer vision, and vision-language models, with a focus on autonomous navigation and locomotion for robotics.

I develop RL pipelines and train agents in simulation using LocoMuJoCo and NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and I love exploring transformer-based models, vision-language grounding, policy learning, and sim-to-real transfer. Whether it's dynamic path planning or low-level motor control, I'm drawn to challenges at the intersection of perception, language, and control.

Outside of research I train Taekwondo 🥋 and hike 🥾 — slowly working toward summiting a mountain 🏔️.
